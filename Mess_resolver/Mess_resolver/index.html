The Problem

A Small Problem Can Become a Big Problem

Many students face situations where reaching the mess or hostel gate is simply not possible.

🍽️

Food Access

Students need to physically reach the mess and scan their assigned QR code to collect their meal — every single time.

🤒

Sick or Injured Students

A student who is sick or injured may not be physically capable of reaching the mess, leaving them without food.

📦

Delivery Collection

Students generally need to reach the hostel gate to collect their food or package deliveries — impossible when bedridden.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Students Without Support

Not every student has friends nearby who can collect food or packages on their behalf when they need it most.

⚠️

Being alone should never mean staying hungry or missing an essential delivery.

Simple Process

How BennettCare Works

Four simple steps from request to delivery.

01
📱

Step 1 — Request

The student opens BennettCare on their device and submits an assistance request. They fill in their name, enrollment number, hostel, room number, and the type of help needed.

  • Choose assistance type
  • Add special instructions
  • Specify preferred time
02
✅

Step 2 — Verification

The platform verifies the student's basic details and the nature of the request to ensure authenticity and prevent misuse. Only enrolled Bennett University students can submit requests.

  • Enrollment number validation
  • Request type categorization
  • Priority classification
03
🙋

Step 3 — Volunteer Accepts

A nearby, available, verified student volunteer receives the request notification and accepts it. Volunteers are pre-registered students who have agreed to the platform's code of conduct.

  • Nearest available volunteer notified
  • Volunteer accepts and confirms
  • Student receives volunteer details
04
🏠

Step 4 — Assistance Delivered

The volunteer collects the food from the mess or the package from the gate and delivers it directly to the student's hostel room. The student confirms receipt, completing the request.

  • Volunteer collects food/package
  • Delivers to hostel room
  • Student confirms receipt
  • Request marked complete

Trust & Safety

🔒 Safety Comes First

BennettCare is built with student safety as its highest priority.

🪪

Verified Students Only

Only verified Bennett University enrolled students can participate as request-makers or volunteers.

🔐

Credential Verification

Student identity is verified using enrollment credentials before any request or volunteer activity.

🙈

Minimal Data Access

Volunteers do not access unnecessary personal information. Requests contain only essential details.

✅

Delivery Confirmation

Package handover requires confirmation from the recipient. Food delivery has a two-way confirmation mechanism.

❌

Request Cancellation

Students can cancel their requests at any time before delivery is completed.

🚨

Report Suspicious Activity

Any suspicious activity or misuse of the platform can be immediately reported and reviewed.

📜

Accountability History

A complete history of all assistance requests is maintained for accountability and review.

👮

Admin Oversight

An admin dashboard allows university staff to monitor all requests, volunteers, and flagged activities.

🆘

Need Immediate Help?

BennettCare is intended for non-medical campus assistance. For serious medical emergencies, students should immediately contact the university's official emergency, security, or medical services.

⚠️ BennettCare is NOT a replacement for emergency medical services.

📞 Contact Campus Security 🏥 Contact Medical Services

About BennettCare

A student-driven prototype for a more inclusive campus at Bennett University.

About the Project

BennettCare is a student-driven prototype created to explore how technology can solve everyday accessibility and assistance challenges faced by hostel students at Bennett University, Greater Noida.

The project demonstrates that a structured, digital platform can bridge the gap between students who temporarily cannot access campus facilities and willing, verified student volunteers who can help.

🎯 Core Mission

"If you cannot reach the mess or the gate, the help can reach you."

This project is a proposed student solution/prototype and is not an official Bennett University service unless formally approved and integrated by the university.
